MERIDIAN ACADEMY — ADMISSION ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
Document ID: MA-ELIG-2026-01
Version: 2.6
Effective for cohorts starting on or after: 2026-02-01
Owner: Admissions Office
Review cycle: Half-yearly

================================================================================
1. HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT
================================================================================

1.1 Every applicant must satisfy the General Eligibility Requirements in
Section 2 and the Program-Specific Requirements in Section 3 for the program
applied to. Failing either results in rejection.

1.2 Where an applicant does not meet a program's requirements, admissions
counsellors are authorised to recommend an alternative program for which the
applicant does qualify. Counsellors are not authorised to waive requirements.
Waivers are granted only under Section 5.

================================================================================
2. GENERAL ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS (ALL PROGRAMS)
================================================================================

2.1 Age. The applicant must be at least 18 years of age on the Cohort Start Date.
There is no upper age limit.

2.2 Education floor. The applicant must have completed higher secondary
education (Class 12 or equivalent). A university degree is not required for every
program — see Section 3.

2.3 English proficiency. All instruction is in English. Applicants must be able
to follow technical instruction in English. This is assessed during the
application and, where applicable, the screening call. No formal test score such
as IELTS or TOEFL is required.

2.4 Meridian Aptitude Test. Every applicant must attempt the Meridian Aptitude
Test (MAT) and meet the minimum score for the program applied to. MAT scores are
valid for 12 months from the test date and may be reused across applications
within that period.

2.5 Hardware and connectivity. The applicant must have access to a laptop or
desktop meeting the minimum specification: 8 GB RAM, dual-core processor from
2018 or later, 10 Mbps internet, webcam, and headset. Mobile-only applicants are
not eligible.

2.6 Time availability. The applicant must be able to commit 15 to 18 hours per
week, including attendance at live classes held on weekday evenings and Saturday
mornings, IST.

2.7 Identity and document verification. The applicant must provide a
government-issued photo identity document and, where a program requires work
experience or a degree, supporting documentation. Documents must be uploaded
before the Cohort Start Date. Enrolment is provisional until verification
completes.

================================================================================
3. PROGRAM-SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 SEF-101 — SOFTWARE ENGINEERING FOUNDATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Minimum MAT score:            60 out of 100
Degree requirement:           None. Class 12 or equivalent is sufficient.
Work experience requirement:  None. Open to students and career changers.
Prior coding requirement:     None. This is a true beginner program.
Screening call:               Not required.
Additional notes:             SEF-101 is the only Meridian program open to
                              applicants with no degree and no prior coding
                              exposure. Applicants who fail to meet FSWD-201 or
                              DSML-301 requirements are commonly redirected here.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2 FSWD-201 — FULL-STACK WEB DEVELOPMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Minimum MAT score:            68 out of 100
Degree requirement:           A completed bachelor's degree in any discipline,
                              OR current enrolment in the final year of a
                              bachelor's degree.
Work experience requirement:  None.
Prior coding requirement:     At least 6 months of demonstrable coding exposure.
                              Acceptable evidence includes a public code
                              repository, a completed online course with a
                              verifiable certificate, prior coursework in
                              programming, or completion of SEF-101.
Screening call:               Not required.
Additional notes:             Final-year students must submit proof of degree
                              completion within 6 months of their Cohort Start
                              Date. Failure to do so does not affect enrolment
                              but does block Placement Assurance eligibility.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3 DSML-301 — DATA SCIENCE AND MACHINE LEARNING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Minimum MAT score:            75 out of 100
Degree requirement:           A completed bachelor's degree. The degree must
                              include at least one course in mathematics,
                              statistics, or quantitative methods, evidenced by a
                              transcript. Degrees in engineering, mathematics,
                              statistics, economics, physics, computer science,
                              and commerce normally satisfy this automatically.
Work experience requirement:  At least 12 months of full-time professional work
                              experience in any field. Internships do not count.
Prior coding requirement:     Working familiarity with at least one programming
                              language. Python is strongly preferred.
Screening call:               Required. 30-minute technical screening covering
                              quantitative reasoning and basic programming.
Additional notes:             Applicants without the mathematics prerequisite may
                              satisfy it by completing the free Meridian
                              Quantitative Bridge module and scoring 70 or above
                              on its final assessment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.4 DCE-401 — DEVOPS AND CLOUD ENGINEERING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Minimum MAT score:            70 out of 100
Degree requirement:           A completed bachelor's degree in any discipline,
                              OR a completed three-year diploma in engineering
                              or computer applications.
Work experience requirement:  At least 12 months of professional experience in
                              an information technology role. Acceptable roles
                              include software development, IT support, system
                              administration, network operations, quality
                              assurance, and technical operations.
Prior coding requirement:     Comfort with the Linux command line and basic
                              scripting in Bash or Python.
Screening call:               Required. 30-minute technical screening covering
                              operating system fundamentals and networking
                              basics.
Additional notes:             Non-IT professionals with strong Linux skills may
                              request an exception under Section 5.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.5 ASD-501 — ADVANCED SYSTEMS DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Minimum MAT score:            Not applicable. The MAT is waived for ASD-501.
Degree requirement:           None, provided the work experience requirement is
                              satisfied.
Work experience requirement:  At least 48 months (4 years) of professional
                              software engineering experience, of which at least
                              24 months must involve building or operating
                              production backend systems.
Prior coding requirement:     Professional fluency in at least one backend
                              language.
Screening call:               Required, and it is substantive. A 45-minute
                              technical interview with a senior instructor
                              covering a past system the applicant has built.
                              This call is a genuine filter; approximately 40% of
                              ASD-501 applicants are declined at this stage.
Additional notes:             ASD-501 is a senior specialisation, not a career
                              switching program. It does not include placement
                              services and is not eligible for Placement
                              Assurance. Applicants seeking placement support
                              should not enrol in ASD-501.

5. WAIVERS AND EXCEPTIONS
================================================================================

5.1 A waiver of any single program-specific requirement may be granted by the
Admissions Committee. Waivers of two or more requirements for the same applicant
are not granted.

5.2 The MAT minimum score may be reduced by at most 5 points, and only where the
applicant demonstrates exceptional evidence in another dimension, such as a
substantial open-source contribution history or a relevant professional
certification.

5.3 The work experience requirement for DSML-301 and DCE-401 may be waived for
applicants holding a master's degree in a quantitative or computing discipline.

5.4 The ASD-501 experience requirement is not waivable under any circumstances.

5.5 Waiver requests are submitted by the admissions counsellor on the applicant's
behalf and are decided within 5 business days. The decision is final and is not
subject to appeal.

================================================================================
6. RE-APPLICATION AFTER REJECTION
================================================================================

6.1 A rejected applicant may re-apply to the same program after 90 calendar days,
or immediately to a different program for which they qualify.

6.2 A rejected applicant may retake the MAT once after the mandatory 21-day
cooling period. A third MAT attempt in the same calendar year is not permitted.

6.3 Rejection at the ASD-501 screening call does not bar an application to
DCE-401 or DSML-301, and the ASD-501 screening outcome is not shared with those
admissions tracks.

================================================================================
7. INTERNATIONAL APPLICANTS
================================================================================

7.1 Applicants located outside India are eligible to enrol in all five programs
and are assessed against the same criteria in Sections 2 and 3.

7.2 Foreign qualifications are accepted. Equivalence is assessed by the
Admissions Office; no third-party evaluation report is required.

7.3 International applicants must be able to attend live classes scheduled in
IST. No alternative time-zone batches are offered.

7.4 International applicants receive career coaching but do not receive hiring
partner referrals and are not eligible for Placement Assurance, because the
hiring partner network covers roles in India only.

7.5 International payments are accepted in INR by international card or bank
transfer. EMI financing through Zenith Capital and Arcline Finance requires an
Indian bank account and Indian credit history, and is therefore unavailable to
most international applicants.
