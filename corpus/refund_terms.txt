================================================================================
1. SCOPE AND DEFINITIONS
================================================================================

1.1 This document sets out the complete refund, withdrawal, and cancellation
terms for all Meridian Academy programs: SEF-101, FSWD-201, DSML-301, DCE-401,
and ASD-501.

1.2 Definitions used throughout this document:

  "Cohort Start Date" means the calendar date of the first scheduled live class
  of the cohort to which the learner is assigned, regardless of whether the
  learner attended that class.

  "Refund Day Count" means the number of calendar days elapsed since the Cohort
  Start Date, where the Cohort Start Date itself is Day 1.

  "Tuition" means the program fee exclusive of Goods and Services Tax, and after
  the application of any scholarship or upfront-payment discount actually
  granted.

  "Enrolment Deposit" means the INR 15,000 seat-reservation payment.

  "Withdrawal Request" means a written request to withdraw, submitted through
  the support portal or by email to refunds@meridianacademy.example from the
  learner's registered email address.

  "Business Day" means Monday to Friday excluding declared public holidays at
  our Bengaluru registered office.

1.3 The Refund Day Count runs on calendar days and does not pause for approved
mid-program pauses, medical leave, festival breaks, or platform downtime. This
is a deliberate policy choice and is not subject to agent discretion.

================================================================================
2. THE REFUND SCHEDULE
================================================================================

2.1 The refund payable on an approved Withdrawal Request is determined solely by
the Refund Day Count on the date the Withdrawal Request is received. The
following schedule applies to all programs:

  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Refund Day Count       Tuition refunded      Deductions
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Day 1 to Day 7         100%                  Less INR 5,000 administrative
  (Cooling-off Period)                         fee. GST fully refunded.
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Day 8 to Day 30        70%                   Administrative fee waived.
                                               GST fully refunded.
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Day 31 to Day 60       40%                   Administrative fee waived.
                                               GST NOT refunded.
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Day 61 onwards         0%                    No refund of any component.
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.2 Worked example. A learner enrols in SEF-101 at a tuition of INR 2,10,000
plus GST of INR 37,800, paid upfront without discount, total INR 2,47,800. The
learner submits a Withdrawal Request on Day 22.

  Refund Day Count 22 falls in the Day 8 to Day 30 band.
  Tuition refund   = 70% of INR 2,10,000 = INR 1,47,000
  GST refund       = full INR 37,800
  Administrative fee = INR 0 (waived in this band)
  Total refund payable = INR 1,84,800

2.3 Worked example, later withdrawal. The same learner withdrawing on Day 45:

  Refund Day Count 45 falls in the Day 31 to Day 60 band.
  Tuition refund   = 40% of INR 2,10,000 = INR 84,000
  GST refund       = INR 0 (not refundable in this band)
  Total refund payable = INR 84,000

2.4 The INR 5,000 administrative fee in the cooling-off band covers payment
gateway charges, onboarding, and issued materials. It is deducted from the
tuition refund, not from the GST refund.

================================================================================
3. PRE-START CANCELLATION
================================================================================

3.1 A learner who submits a Withdrawal Request before the Cohort Start Date
receives a 100% refund of all amounts paid, including the Enrolment Deposit and
GST, with no administrative fee deducted. This is a full and unconditional
refund.

3.2 Section 3.1 applies regardless of how far in advance the payment was made
and regardless of whether onboarding material has been issued.

3.3 If Meridian Academy cancels or postpones a cohort by more than 21 calendar
days, affected learners may elect either a full unconditional refund under
Section 3.1 or a transfer to any future cohort with no deferral fee.

================================================================================
4. GST TREATMENT
================================================================================

4.1 GST is charged at 18% on the tuition component at the time of payment.

4.2 GST is refunded in full for Withdrawal Requests with a Refund Day Count of
30 or less, and for all pre-start cancellations under Section 3.

4.3 GST is not refundable for Withdrawal Requests with a Refund Day Count of 31
or more. This is because the tax has been remitted to the authorities for the
relevant filing period and cannot be reclaimed by Meridian Academy.

4.4 GST is never refunded on the INR 5,000 administrative fee.

================================================================================
5. REFUNDS WHERE FINANCING IS INVOLVED
================================================================================

5.1 Where tuition was financed through a partner NBFC (Zenith Capital or
Arcline Finance), the refund is not paid to the learner in the first instance.
Meridian Academy remits the approved refund amount directly to the NBFC to be
applied against the outstanding loan principal.

5.2 If the refund amount exceeds the outstanding loan balance, the NBFC closes
the loan and the surplus is remitted to the learner's registered bank account.

5.3 If the refund amount is less than the outstanding loan balance, the loan is
reduced but not closed. The learner remains contractually liable to the NBFC for
the remaining balance, together with any interest and fees accrued under the loan
agreement. Meridian Academy has no authority to waive amounts owed to an NBFC.

5.4 Interest already paid to the NBFC on tenures of 18 or 24 months is not
refundable by Meridian Academy under any circumstances, as Meridian Academy does
not receive that interest.

5.5 Subvention costs absorbed by Meridian Academy on 0% interest tenures (6, 9,
and 12 months) are treated as a cost of sale and are not deducted from the
learner's refund.

================================================================================
6. SCHOLARSHIPS AND DISCOUNTS IN REFUND CALCULATIONS
================================================================================

6.1 Refunds are computed on Tuition as defined in Section 1.2, that is, on the
discounted amount actually payable, not on the list price.

6.2 Worked example. A learner on DSML-301 with list tuition INR 3,40,000
receives a 20% Career Restart Scholarship, making Tuition INR 2,72,000. A
withdrawal on Day 12 yields a tuition refund of 70% of INR 2,72,000 =
INR 1,90,400, plus full GST refund on the GST actually paid.

6.3 A learner who paid upfront and received the 10% upfront discount has their
refund computed on the post-discount amount. The discount is not clawed back and
is not added back for refund purposes.

================================================================================
7. NON-REFUNDABLE ITEMS
================================================================================

7.1 The following are never refundable, in any band, including during the
cooling-off period:

  a) The INR 7,500 deferral processing fee.
  b) Fees for optional add-on services purchased separately, including the
     Interview Intensive workshop and one-to-one career coaching packages.
  c) The cost of physical materials already dispatched, where the learner does
     not return them in resaleable condition within 14 days.
  d) Payment gateway charges on international card transactions, where these
     exceed INR 5,000; in that case the actual charge is deducted instead of the
     standard administrative fee.

7.2 The Enrolment Deposit is refundable only under Section 3 (pre-start
cancellation). Once the Cohort Start Date has passed, the deposit is treated as
tuition already paid and is subject to the Section 2 schedule.

================================================================================
8. TERMINATION FOR CONDUCT
================================================================================

8.1 Where enrolment is terminated by Meridian Academy for a code of conduct
violation, including plagiarism, impersonation during proctored assessment, or
redistribution of course material, no refund of any kind is payable, irrespective
of the Refund Day Count.

8.2 A learner may appeal a conduct termination within 14 calendar days to the
Academic Integrity Committee. A successful appeal restores enrolment; it does not
convert the case into a refund.

================================================================================
9. PROCESS AND TIMELINES
================================================================================

9.1 Withdrawal Requests must be submitted in writing from the learner's
registered email address. Verbal requests to counsellors, messages in class chat,
and social media messages do not constitute a Withdrawal Request and do not stop
the Refund Day Count.

9.2 The date of receipt is the date the request arrives in our systems, in IST.
A request received at 23:50 IST on Day 30 is treated as Day 30. A request
received at 00:10 IST the following day is treated as Day 31.

9.3 Meridian Academy acknowledges a Withdrawal Request within 2 Business Days
and issues a refund decision with a computed amount within 7 Business Days.

9.4 Approved refunds are disbursed within 21 Business Days of the decision, to
the original payment instrument. Where the original instrument is closed or
invalid, disbursement is made by bank transfer to a verified account and may take
an additional 10 Business Days.

9.5 Platform access, including recordings and community channels, is revoked on
the date the refund decision is issued, not on the date funds are received.

================================================================================
10. DISPUTES
================================================================================

10.1 A learner who disputes a refund computation may escalate to
grievance@meridianacademy.example within 30 calendar days of the refund decision.
Escalations are reviewed by the Finance and Compliance lead and answered within
10 Business Days.

10.2 Unresolved disputes are subject to arbitration seated in Bengaluru under the
Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, before a sole arbitrator appointed by
Meridian Academy and agreed by the learner.

10.3 Nothing in this document limits a learner's statutory rights under
applicable consumer protection law.
