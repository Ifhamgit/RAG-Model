SECTION 1: ABOUT MERIDIAN ACADEMY
================================================================================

Q1.1 What is Meridian Academy?
Meridian Academy is an online, instructor-led upskilling institution for working
professionals and career starters in software engineering, data science, and
cloud infrastructure. We run structured, cohort-based programs with live classes,
mentor sessions, graded assessments, and placement support. We are not a
self-paced video library — attendance and assessment completion are tracked and
are a precondition for placement services.

Q1.2 Where is Meridian Academy located?
Meridian Academy is a fully remote institution. Our registered office is at
Level 6, Kestrel Tower, Outer Ring Road, Bengaluru 560103. All classes are
delivered online over our learning platform. We do not operate physical campuses
and we do not offer offline classroom batches.

Q1.3 Which programs do you offer?
We currently offer five programs:
  - SEF-101  Software Engineering Foundations      (9 months)
  - FSWD-201 Full-Stack Web Development            (11 months)
  - DSML-301 Data Science & Machine Learning       (13 months)
  - DCE-401  DevOps & Cloud Engineering            (8 months)
  - ASD-501  Advanced Systems Design               (6 months)
Full syllabi, weekly time commitments, and instructor profiles are in the Course
Brochure (document MA-BROCHURE-2026-02).

Q1.4 Are Meridian Academy programs accredited or degree-granting?
No. Meridian Academy is not a university and does not award degrees, diplomas
recognised by the UGC, or any government-accredited qualification. On completion
you receive a Meridian Academy Certificate of Completion, which is an industry
certificate, not an academic degree. Learners who need a recognised degree should
not enrol expecting one.

Q1.5 What language are classes taught in?
All live classes, recordings, assessments, and mentor sessions are conducted in
English. Doubt-resolution sessions are available in English and Hindi.

SECTION 2: ADMISSIONS AND ENROLMENT
================================================================================

Q2.1 How do I apply?
Applications are made through the Meridian Academy website. The process is:
  Step 1 — Submit the online application form (10 minutes).
  Step 2 — Take the Meridian Aptitude Test (MAT), a 60-minute online test.
  Step 3 — For DSML-301, DCE-401, and ASD-501, attend a 30-minute technical
           screening call with an admissions counsellor.
  Step 4 — Receive an admission decision within 5 business days of Step 3.
  Step 5 — Accept the offer and pay the enrolment deposit to reserve a seat.

Q2.2 What is the Meridian Aptitude Test (MAT)?
The MAT is a 60-minute proctored online test covering quantitative aptitude,
logical reasoning, and basic programming logic. It is scored out of 100. The
minimum qualifying score varies by program and is listed in the Eligibility
Criteria document (MA-ELIG-2026-01). You may retake the MAT once after a
mandatory 21-day cooling period. A third attempt is not permitted in the same
calendar year.

Q2.3 Is there an enrolment deposit? Is it refundable?
Yes. The enrolment deposit is INR 15,000 and is required to reserve a seat in a
cohort. The deposit is adjusted against your total tuition. It is fully
refundable if you withdraw before your cohort's first live class. After the
first live class, the deposit is treated as part of tuition and is governed by
the Refund Terms document (MA-REFUND-2026-01).

Q2.4 How large are the cohorts?
Cohorts are capped at 120 learners. Learners are split into mentor groups of at
most 12 for mentor sessions and project reviews.

Q2.5 How often do new cohorts start?
SEF-101 and FSWD-201 start monthly. DSML-301 and DCE-401 start every two months.
ASD-501 starts quarterly (January, April, July, October).

Q2.6 Can I defer my admission to a later cohort?
Yes, once. Each learner is permitted one deferral, for a maximum of two cohorts
forward from the originally assigned cohort. A deferral processing fee of
INR 7,500 applies. Deferral requests must be submitted at least 14 calendar days
before the original cohort's start date. Requests made inside 14 days are treated
as a withdrawal and fall under the standard refund schedule.

Q2.7 I missed my cohort start date. What happens?
If you have not attended any live class and you contact support within 7 calendar
days of the cohort start, we will attempt to move you to the next available
cohort at no charge. Beyond 7 days, the standard deferral fee of INR 7,500
applies and your refund clock has already started.

SECTION 3: FEES, PAYMENTS, AND FINANCING
================================================================================

Q3.1 How much do the programs cost?
Program fees range from INR 1,95,000 to INR 3,40,000 exclusive of GST. Exact
per-program pricing, including all EMI tenures and discounts, is maintained in
the structured pricing document (MA-PRICING-2026-03). Support agents must quote
from that document, never from memory, as prices are revised quarterly.

Q3.2 Is GST included in the advertised price?
No. All advertised program fees are exclusive of GST. GST is charged at 18% on
the tuition amount and is added at checkout. On an INR 2,10,000 program, GST of
INR 37,800 applies, making the payable total INR 2,47,800.

Q3.3 Do you offer EMI or instalment plans?
Yes. We offer EMI tenures of 6, 9, 12, 18, and 24 months through our partner
non-banking financial companies, Zenith Capital and Arcline Finance. Tenures of
6, 9, and 12 months are offered at 0% interest, with Meridian Academy absorbing
the subvention cost. Tenures of 18 and 24 months carry interest charged by the
lender at 12.5% and 13.9% per annum respectively. Meridian Academy does not
receive any part of that interest.

Q3.4 Do I need a credit check for EMI?
Yes. EMI approval is granted by the partner NBFC, not by Meridian Academy. The
NBFC runs a credit check and may decline an application. If your EMI application
is declined by both partners, you may pay upfront, choose a shorter tenure, or
apply with a co-applicant who has an income record.

Q3.5 Is there a discount for paying upfront?
Yes. Paying the full tuition upfront before the cohort start date earns a 10%
discount on the tuition component. The discount does not apply to GST, and does
not stack with EMI plans.

Q3.6 Do you offer an Income Share Agreement (ISA) or pay-after-placement option?
No. Meridian Academy discontinued all Income Share Agreement products with effect
from 1 January 2026. Learners who signed an ISA before that date continue under
their original contract terms; those contracts are unaffected. No new ISA
enrolments are accepted, and we do not offer any pay-after-placement scheme.

Q3.7 What scholarships are available?
Three scholarships are offered:
  - Merit Scholarship: up to 30% off tuition, based on MAT score of 85 or above.
  - Women in Tech Scholarship: flat 15% off tuition.
  - Career Restart Scholarship: flat 20% off tuition, for applicants returning
    to work after a documented career break of 12 months or longer.
Scholarships may be combined, but total stacked discount is capped at 35% of
tuition. Scholarship discounts do not apply to GST or to the enrolment deposit.

Q3.8 Does taking a scholarship affect my placement benefits?
Yes, in one specific case. Learners receiving a total scholarship discount
greater than 30% of tuition are not eligible for the Placement Assurance refund
programme. They still receive full placement support services. See the Placement
Policy document (MA-PLACE-2026-02) for details.

Q3.9 Can I change my payment plan after enrolling?
You may switch from EMI to upfront payment at any time by settling the
outstanding principal with the NBFC directly; the 10% upfront discount does not
apply retroactively. You may not switch from upfront to EMI after payment.

Q3.10 What happens if I miss an EMI payment?
The EMI contract is between you and the partner NBFC. Missed payments are subject
to the NBFC's late fees and are reported to credit bureaus by the NBFC. Meridian
Academy suspends platform access after 30 days of confirmed non-payment and
restores it within 48 hours of the NBFC confirming that the account is current.

SECTION 4: CLASSES, ATTENDANCE, AND ASSESSMENTS
================================================================================

Q4.1 When are classes held?
Live classes are held on weekday evenings from 8:00 PM to 10:00 PM IST, and on
Saturdays from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM IST. Exact weekly schedules vary by program
and are published two weeks before cohort start.

Q4.2 Are classes recorded?
Yes. Every live class is recorded and made available on the platform within 12
hours. Recordings remain accessible for 12 months after your program end date.

Q4.3 Does watching a recording count towards attendance?
Partially. Watching a full recording within 7 days of the live session counts as
0.5 attendance credit. Attending live counts as 1.0 credit. This matters because
placement eligibility has an attendance threshold — see Q5.2.

Q4.4 What is the weekly time commitment?
Plan for 15 to 18 hours per week: approximately 7 hours of live class, 4 hours of
assignments, 3 hours of project work, and 2 hours of mentor or doubt sessions.
DSML-301 and ASD-501 trend towards the upper end of that range.

Q4.5 What assessments are there?
Each program has 6 module assessments, 2 or more capstone projects depending on
the program, and a final technical evaluation. Module assessments are graded
pass/fail with a 60% pass mark. You get two reattempt opportunities per module
assessment at no cost.

Q4.6 What happens if I fail a module assessment three times?
You are moved to Academic Support, which pairs you with a mentor for four weeks
of remediation at no additional cost. You may then attempt the assessment a
fourth time. If that attempt fails, you may repeat the module with the next
cohort at no tuition cost, but your placement timeline shifts accordingly.

Q4.7 Can I pause my program mid-way?
Yes. A mid-program pause of up to 8 weeks is permitted once per learner for
documented medical or family emergencies. Submit a pause request through the
support portal with supporting documentation. Pauses are not granted for work
commitments or travel. Your refund window does not pause — see the Refund Terms.

SECTION 5: PLACEMENT AND CAREER SERVICES
================================================================================

Q5.1 Do you guarantee a job?
No, not universally. Meridian Academy provides placement support to all learners
who clear the Placement Readiness Gate. Separately, a conditional refund product
called Placement Assurance is available only on FSWD-201 and DSML-301, and only
to learners who meet strict participation conditions. It is a conditional refund
promise, not a job guarantee. Read the Placement Policy (MA-PLACE-2026-02) in
full before relying on it.

Q5.2 What is the Placement Readiness Gate?
The Placement Readiness Gate, or PRG, is the checkpoint that unlocks placement
services. To clear it you must have at least 85% attendance credit, have passed
all 6 module assessments, and have submitted and had approved at least 2 capstone
projects. Learners who do not clear the PRG receive career coaching but are not
referred to hiring partners.

Q5.3 How long does placement support last?
Placement support runs for 12 months from the date you clear the PRG. It is not
12 months from your program end date. Learners who clear the PRG late therefore
still receive a full 12-month window.

Q5.4 What kind of companies hire from Meridian Academy?
Our hiring partner network includes product startups, mid-size SaaS companies,
IT services firms, and a small number of large product companies. A partial
partner list and historical placement statistics appear in the Placement Policy.

Q5.5 Is placement support available to learners outside India?
Placement support and hiring partner referrals are available for roles in India
only. Learners located outside India may enrol and complete the program and
receive career coaching, résumé review, and interview preparation, but will not
receive hiring partner referrals and are not eligible for Placement Assurance.

SECTION 6: REFUNDS, WITHDRAWALS, AND CANCELLATIONS
================================================================================

Q6.1 What is your refund policy in brief?
Refunds are tiered by how many days have passed since your cohort's first live
class: 100% minus an administrative fee within 7 days, 70% from day 8 to day 30,
40% from day 31 to day 60, and no refund after day 60. The authoritative and
complete terms, including GST treatment and processing timelines, are in the
Refund Terms document (MA-REFUND-2026-01). Support agents must quote that
document, not this summary.

Q6.2 How long does a refund take to reach me?
Approved refunds are processed within 21 business days of approval to the
original payment instrument.

Q6.3 I am on an EMI plan and want to withdraw. What happens to my loan?
Your refund is computed on the tuition as normal, but the refund amount is paid
first to the partner NBFC to reduce or close your outstanding loan. Any surplus
after the loan is closed is paid to you. If the refund is smaller than the
outstanding loan, you remain liable to the NBFC for the balance.

SECTION 7: TECHNICAL AND ACCOUNT SUPPORT
================================================================================

Q7.1 What are your support hours?
Learner support operates 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM IST, Monday to Saturday. We are
closed on Sundays and on 12 declared public holidays per year.

Q7.2 What is your response time commitment?
We respond to all support tickets within 24 hours of receipt during operating
days. Tickets raised on a Saturday evening are answered by Monday evening.
Billing and refund tickets carry a 48-hour first-response target because they
require finance team review.

Q7.3 How do I contact support?
Raise a ticket from the Help section of the learning platform, or email
support@meridianacademy.example. Phone support is available to enrolled learners
only, on the number shown in the platform, during support hours.

Q7.4 What are the technical requirements for classes?
A laptop or desktop with at least 8 GB RAM, a dual-core processor from 2018 or
later, a stable internet connection of at least 10 Mbps, a working webcam, and a
headset. DSML-301 learners are provided cloud GPU credits for model training and
do not need a local GPU. Tablets and phones are not supported for assessments or
proctored tests.

Q7.5 I cannot access my recordings. What should I do?
Recording access is suspended automatically if your payment account is in arrears
by more than 30 days, or if your program end date was more than 12 months ago.
If neither applies, raise a technical ticket with your cohort ID and the specific
class date.

================================================================================
SECTION 8: DATA, PRIVACY, AND CONDUCT
================================================================================

Q8.1 Is class attendance monitored?
Yes. Attendance is recorded automatically by the platform based on session join
and leave timestamps. A learner must be present for at least 75% of a session's
duration for it to count as attended.

Q8.2 Are proctored assessments recorded?
Yes. The MAT and the final technical evaluation are proctored with webcam and
screen recording. Recordings are retained for 180 days and then deleted.

Q8.3 What is the code of conduct?
Plagiarism in assignments or capstone projects, sharing assessment content, and
impersonation during proctored tests are treated as serious violations. A first
confirmed violation results in a written warning and a zero on the affected
assessment. A second results in termination of enrolment without refund.

Q8.4 Can I share my course material with others?
No. All course material, recordings, and assessment content are licensed to you
personally and may not be redistributed, resold, or uploaded elsewhere. Detected
redistribution results in immediate termination of enrolment without refund.
